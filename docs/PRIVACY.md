# Privacy Policy — WE, HUMANITY Studio

*Last updated: November 17, 2026*

## 1. Overview
**WE, HUMANITY Studio** ("the Service") is an internal publishing tool for the **WE, HUMANITY** (`@by.wehumanity`) editorial series. This Privacy Policy explains what data is accessed when connecting social accounts such as TikTok or Instagram.

## 2. Data We Collect & Use
- **Account Authorization Tokens:** When you authorize TikTok or Instagram via OAuth, the Service stores your access/refresh tokens locally on your machine solely to upload and publish your chosen carousel slides and captions.
- **Basic Profile Info (`user.info.basic`):** Used only to verify the connected account identity in the Studio interface.
- **Media Assets:** Rendered slide PNGs are temporarily uploaded to Firebase Storage only long enough for the target social platform to ingest them, and are deleted immediately afterward.

## 3. Data Sharing & Selling
We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal data, OAuth tokens, or account information with third parties, except as strictly required to transmit your authorized posts to the connected platform APIs (TikTok, Meta/Instagram, Firebase).

## 4. Data Retention & Revocation
- OAuth tokens are stored locally on the operator's host and can be deleted or revoked at any time via your TikTok or Instagram account security settings.

## 5. Contact
For privacy questions or data removal requests, contact the project maintainer via `https://github.com/samuelliam/we-humanity`.
